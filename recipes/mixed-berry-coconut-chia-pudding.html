Mixed Berry & Coconut Chia Pudding

A layered dessert featuring creamy coconut chia pudding topped with a vibrant mixed berry compote and fresh fruit.

Prep Time15 min
Cook Time1 hr 10 min
Servings4
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1/4 cup chia seeds
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup
  • 1 cup mixed berries (strawberries, raspberries, blueberries)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Fresh mint leaves
  • Edible flowers (optional)
  • Fresh strawberries, sliced
  • Fresh raspberries
  • Fresh blueberries

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together coconut milk, chia seeds, maple syrup, lemon juice, and vanilla extract until well combined.
  2. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight until the chia pudding has thickened.
  3. While the pudding sets, prepare the berry compote by combining mixed berries and lemon juice in a small saucepan over medium heat.
  4. Simmer the berries for 5-7 minutes until they break down and form a sauce, then remove from heat and let cool.
  5. To assemble, spoon a layer of chia pudding into a clear glass bowl, followed by a layer of berry compote.
  6. Repeat the layers, finishing with a dollop of chia pudding on top.
  7. Garnish with fresh berries, mint leaves, and edible flowers for a vibrant presentation.
  8. Serve chilled and enjoy the refreshing, healthy dessert.
