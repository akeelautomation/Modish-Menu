Raspberry Cream Rolls

Delicate yellow crepes filled with fluffy cream and fresh raspberries, dusted with powdered sugar.

Prep Time20 min
Cook Time30 min
Servings4
DifficultyMedium

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar (for filling)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup fresh raspberries
  • Powdered sugar for dusting

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together flour, eggs, milk, melted butter, and salt until smooth.
  2. Cover and refrigerate batter for 30 minutes.
  3. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and lightly grease with butter.
  4. Pour 1/4 cup batter into the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom evenly.
  5. Cook for 1-2 minutes until golden, then flip and cook for another 30 seconds.
  6. Transfer to a plate and repeat with remaining batter, stacking the crepes as you go.
  7. In a separate bowl, whip heavy cream with 2 tbsp powdered sugar and vanilla until stiff peaks form.
  8. Spread a thin layer of cream on a crepe, add a few raspberries, and roll tightly.
  9. Repeat with remaining crepes and raspberries.
  10. Slice the rolls into 1-inch thick pieces and arrange on a serving plate.
  11. Dust with powdered sugar and garnish with fresh raspberries and mint leaves.
