Mediterranean Orzo Salad

A vibrant orzo salad with cherry tomatoes, cucumbers, Kalamata olives, feta cheese, and a lemon-herb vinaigrette.

Prep Time15 min
Cook Time20 min
Servings4
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Orzo
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Cucumbers
  • Kalamata olives
  • Red onion
  • Feta cheese
  • Fresh parsley
  • Lemon juice
  • Olive oil
  • Dried oregano
  • Minced garlic

Instructions

  1. Cook orzo according to package instructions and cool completely.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together lemon juice, olive oil, dried oregano, and minced garlic to make the dressing.
  3. Toss the cooled orzo with the dressing.
  4. Gently fold in cherry tomatoes, cucumbers, Kalamata olives, red onion, feta cheese, and fresh parsley.
  5. Garnish with a lemon wedge before serving.
