Ground Beef, Crispy Potatoes, Sharp Cheddar, Fresh Avocado

A hearty summer dinner featuring seasoned ground beef, roasted russet potato cubes, sharp cheddar cheese, fresh avocado, tomatoes, and green onions, topped with sour cream.

Prep Time15 min
Cook Time30 min
Servings4
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 4 russet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1 ripe avocado, diced
  • 1 cup diced vine-ripened tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped green onions
  • 1/2 cup sour cream

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Toss potato cubes with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Spread potatoes on a baking sheet and roast for 25-30 minutes until golden and crispy.
  4. While potatoes roast, cook ground beef in a skillet over medium heat until browned, about 8-10 minutes.
  5. Drain excess grease from the beef.
  6. In a large bowl, combine roasted potatoes, cooked ground beef, shredded cheddar cheese, diced avocado, diced tomatoes, and chopped green onions.
  7. Top with a generous drizzle of sour cream.
  8. Serve immediately in a bowl.
